# Data-Analysis-Dashboard
this interactive Excel dashboard analyzes K-Store's 2025 sales data, highlighting key trends in customer demographics, top-performing states, and sales channels to drive data-driven business strategies and optimize retail performance.
K-Store Annual Sales Analysis (2025)
Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive analysis of K-Store's annual data to identify sales trends, customer demographics, and channel performance. The goal was to provide actionable insights that help the business improve its sales strategy for the upcoming year.
Key Questions Answered (Business Problems)
•	Which months saw the highest and lowest sales/orders?
•	Who are our primary customers (Gender and Age Group)?
•	Which sales channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.) contribute most to the revenue?
•	Which states are the top revenue generators?
•	What is the current order fulfillment rate (Order Status)?
Technical Skills Applied
•	Data Cleaning: Handled missing values, standardized category names, and ensured date formatting.
•	Data Processing: Created new columns for Age Group (Adult, Senior, Teenager) and Month using Excel formulas.
•	Data Analysis: Utilized Pivot Tables to aggregate data across multiple dimensions (States, Channels, Gender).
•	Data Visualization: Designed an interactive dashboard using:
o	Combo Charts (Orders vs. Sales).
o	Pie/Donut Charts (Gender distribution and Channel share).
o	Bar Charts (Geographic performance).
o	Slicers (Filtering by Month, Channel, and Category).
Key Insights & Recommendations
•	Top Channels: Amazon and Myntra are the leading contributors, accounting for over 58% of total orders.
•	Customer Persona: Women are the primary buyers, making up 64% of the total sales volume.
•	Geographic Focus: Maharashtra and Karnataka are the highest-performing states; marketing efforts should be intensified here.
•	Target Audience: The "Adult" age group (approx. 35%) is the most active; targeted ad campaigns should focus on this demographic.
•	Operational Health: A high delivery rate of 92% shows strong logistics, though a small percentage of returns/refunds should be investigated for quality control.
